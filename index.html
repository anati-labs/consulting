About

Hands-on cloud leadership

Anati Labs is a consulting practice founded by Anoop Puthankalam, a Cloud Architect and DevOps leader with over 20 years of experience designing, implementing and optimizing scalable infrastructure.

Over the years that has included multi-region cloud transformations, standardized delivery processes, and building DevOps and SRE teams. Most recently it meant leading platform engineering for a GCP-based Kubernetes platform, built end to end on Terraform, delivered with GitOps and backed by production-grade observability, including a full production region migration and the decommissioning of the legacy environment.

The aim is always practical, reliable and cost-efficient cloud solutions that line up with business goals.
